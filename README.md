# GoPreach Planner

Personal ministry planner for Android: My Planner, My Ministry (Searching, Return Visits, Bible Studies) and Territory with barangay borders. Works on the phone, no account needed.

## Download

Direct download of the APK only:

**https://github.com/Eph0714/GoPreach-Planner/releases/download/v1.0.0/GoPreach-Planner-1.0.0.apk**

## Install

1. Open the link on your Android phone and let it download.
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or Files app.
3. Open **GoPreach Planner** and register with your name and category. No username or password.
